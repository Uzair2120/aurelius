YOUR LIFETIME ROADMAP

What to Focus On at Every Age

You don't have to do everything at once. Focus on the single most impactful move for your current stage in life.

IN YOUR 20s & 30s

The Magic of Time

Time is your biggest superpower. Every $100 you invest in your 20s can turn into $1,500+ by the time you retire due to decades of compounding. Just start with whatever you can afford.

IN YOUR 40s

Accelerating Savings

As your salary increases, increase your automatic monthly contributions before lifestyle spending catches up. Pay down any high-interest debt and max out your tax-advantaged accounts.

IN YOUR 50s & 60s

The Home Stretch

Take advantage of IRS "catch-up contributions" to save even more pre-tax dollars. Gradually shift some investments into stable bonds to protect your nest egg from short-term market dips.

CHECKLIST

3 Simple Financial Milestones Everyone Should Hit

🎁

1. Capture Your 401(k) Match

If your employer offers a 3% or 5% match, that is an immediate, guaranteed 100% return on your money. Always contribute at least enough to capture every dollar.

🛡️

2. 3-6 Month Safety Buffer

Before investing aggressively, build an emergency buffer in a high-yield savings account. It prevents you from having to pull money out of investments during tough times.

📝

3. Designate Account Beneficiaries

Log in to your bank and investment accounts and name a primary beneficiary (like a spouse or child). It's 100% free and ensures your assets go directly to loved ones.
