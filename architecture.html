✨ HOW IT WORKS PLAIN-ENGLISH AUTOMATION

Smart money management, simplified for everyone.

Under the hood, Aurelius does the heavy math, tracking, and calculations automatically. Here is how our friendly guidance system keeps your savings safe and growing every day.

THE 4 PILLARS

Your 4 automated financial coaches

Think of Aurelius as four friendly specialists quietly working on your behalf 24/7.

🛡️

The Safety Coach

Protects Your Emergency Buffer

Always ensures you have 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses (rent, groceries, bills) safely set aside in cash. It prevents you from accidentally overspending or risking money you need for daily life.

💬 "Your emergency fund is 100% funded ($10,000). You are completely protected from unexpected life surprises."
💡

The Smart Saver

Finds Hidden Money in Your Budget

Quietly monitors recurring subscriptions and bills. It flags price increases, identifies duplicate streaming services, and suggests painless ways to save an extra $100 to $300 every month.

💬 "We found an unused $35/mo gym membership. Cancelling it saves you $420 this year!"
🎯

The Goal Planner

Turns Dreams into Automatic Habits

Whether you want to buy a new car, take a family vacation, or save a home down payment, the Goal Planner calculates the exact monthly amount needed so you reach your target stress-free.

💬 "At $400/month, your New Car target ($20,000) will be 100% reached in just 14 months."
📈

The Growth Advisor

Harnesses Compound Growth

Instead of letting long-term savings sit in a 0% bank account, the Growth Advisor puts compounding to work through low-cost index funds and safe high-yield accounts.

💬 "Your investments gained +12.4% this year. That is $3,820 added to your wealth without working extra hours."
