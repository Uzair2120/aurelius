🔒 SAFETY & PRIVACY BANK-GRADE PROTECTION

How we keep your money and data 100% safe.

Security shouldn't be confusing. Here is the plain truth about how we protect your personal information, encrypt your balances, and safeguard your financial future.

🔐

256-Bit Bank Encryption

Every byte of data sent between your device and our servers is encrypted using AES-256 and TLS 1.3 encryption — the exact same cryptographic standard mandated for national banks.

🏦

Your Money Stays in Your Name

Aurelius is software, not a high-risk lender. Your deposits remain securely housed in your own name at regulated, insured partner banks. We never lend your money out.

🚫

Zero Data Selling

Many free apps make money by selling your spending habits and personal details to advertisers. We never do this. Your private financial life stays strictly between you and your screen.

📱

Biometric & 2-Factor Access

Log in securely using Face ID, Touch ID, or two-factor authentication codes. Even if someone obtains your password, they cannot access your dashboard without your physical phone.
