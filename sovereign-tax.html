ESSENTIAL STRATEGIES

4 Legal Ways Anyone Can Lower Their Taxes

You don't need complex offshore accounts or million-dollar trusts. These standard, legal tools work for any working individual or family.

💼

1. Traditional 401(k) & IRA

When you contribute money straight from your paycheck into a Traditional 401(k) or IRA, that money isn't counted as taxable income this year. If you make $60,000 and save $5,000, you are only taxed on $55,000.

🏥

2. Health Savings Accounts (HSA)

Often called the "triple tax advantage." Money goes in pre-tax, grows tax-free, and can be withdrawn completely tax-free whenever you spend it on qualified doctor visits, prescriptions, contacts, and dental care.

📋

3. The Standard Deduction

Most people don't need to save receipts in a shoebox. The government gives almost all taxpayers a generous automatic deduction ($14,600+ for single filers, $29,200+ for married couples) with zero paperwork needed.

🔄

4. Automated Tax-Loss Offsets

If a fund temporarily dips during regular market swings, Aurelius can automatically sell that dip to offset taxes on other profits, then reinvest immediately. It's smart, completely automated, and saves you money every tax season.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Taxes

What is the difference between a tax credit and a deduction?

A deduction lowers the amount of income you are taxed on (for example, saving $1,000 might save you $220 in taxes). A tax credit directly reduces your final tax bill dollar-for-dollar (a $1,000 credit saves you a full $1,000 in cash).

Do I need to hire an expensive accountant?

Unless you own multiple complex corporations or cross-border rental portfolios, simple tax software or Aurelius tools are all 95% of people will ever need. It takes less than 30 minutes once a year.

Can Aurelius help me keep track of this?

Yes! Aurelius connects to your bank and retirement accounts to track your contributions in real-time, reminding you of deadlines and showing you exactly how much you're saving on taxes.
